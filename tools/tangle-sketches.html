Tangle Sketches

Five candidate pictures of one knot of feedback, computed from the same data and stacked so they can be compared. A list of loops answers what are the loops; none of these do that, and all of them try to answer what is this thing.

Picking one

1 is the one I would build. It is the only one that keeps the boxes in an order you can follow, it puts feedback in the same left-to-right frame the atlas already uses, and each arc is a loop you can point at. 4 answers a different question and is cheap to keep alongside it: it is the only one that tells you whether the knot is one thing at all.

2 is the one to reach for if the tangle needs to sit inline as a small glyph rather than a full-width panel. 3 is the only one that will not break on a tangle of a thousand boxes. 5 is the answer to a question the others cannot touch — how much of a long loop list is the same story told in different lanes.

All five are drawn from one shared ordering and take a few milliseconds. Nothing here is wired into the atlas yet.
