Sidebar — most user-friendly & accessible layout

The headline defect is a two-level left-edge zigzag: Stages rows have no colour swatch, so their labels sit ~22px left of Streams / Categories rows (which do) — and the section headings step the same way. Scanning down, the label column jumps right at "Streams." Pick an option to see it fixed; the dashed amber guides mark where labels & headings line up (toggle ▥ guides), and ⇄ Split compares against today.

The ladder. ● A — Straighten (CSS-only): one label/heading axis; stages get an inert swatch-slot marker. Fixes the zigzag, nothing else. ● B — + declutter rails: drag handle reveals on hover/focus (swatch owns the left edge); short-chip & count pin into fixed right columns; stronger swatch ring; focus-reachable actions. ● C — + full keyboard/touch a11y: rows become real role=button toggles, touch-reachable actions, ≥24px hit areas (via slop, density unchanged), keyboard reorder.
Recommendation — Option B. A alone fixes the zigzag but leaves the right edge jittering and keeps the noisy always-on drag handle on the column the swatch should own. B gives one straight left axis and two straight right rails at modest cost, preserves Compact density, and folds in the cheap high-value accessibility wins (focus-reachable delete/reclassify — a real WCAG 2.4.7 fix — and a visible swatch ring). Reserve C for when full keyboard/touch operability is in scope. Land them A → B → C.

01What the examination found

Label + heading edges zigzag alignment

Stage labels start at 26px (no swatch); stream/category labels at 48px. Headings step the same way — the calc --title-indent system aligns each section to its own rows, so the sections disagree. The brief's core fix.

Always-on drag handle clutters the left clutter

A low-contrast ⋮⋮ sits in the leftmost column of every row, full-time, for a secondary (reorder) action — pushing the swatch (the real identifier) inward and competing for the left scan edge.

Two jittering right-hand columns hierarchy

Stream rows carry a short-chip (OPS/SALES/SUPPORT) and a count, packed after a variable-width label — so neither forms a vertical column. Stages/streams/categories each show a different right side.

Delete/reclassify are hover-only WCAG 2.4.7

They're opacity:0, revealed only on :hover — but they're real buttons in the tab order, so a keyboard user can focus them while they stay invisible. No :focus-within anywhere. Unreachable on touch.

Tight hit targets & faint affordances a11y

Compact rows ~26px; the swatch ring is ~1.2:1 (a dark user-chosen fill loses its boundary); drag dots are 13px sub-pixel marks. Below comfortable touch / low-vision thresholds.

Verbose headings & centred chevron minor

PRIMARY CATEGORIES · FILL nearly fills the 240px panel; the · FILL/· CHIPS suffix restates what the rows already show. The collapse chevron is centred at the top (edge is more conventional).
