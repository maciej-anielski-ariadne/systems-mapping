Categories → Primary & Secondary

Two classes of category. Primary sets the node's fill (today's behaviour); selecting several primaries blends them into a smooth gradient. Secondary adds small coloured boxes at the bottom-right; each extra secondary adds another box. These are real SVG nodes drawn the way the app draws them (220 px wide, stream stripe on the left, grow-to-fit height), so the gradient previews exactly how an SVG linearGradient fill would render.
